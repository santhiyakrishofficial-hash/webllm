Model Information for KidsZone AI Tutor
=======================================

Model Name:
Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct-q4f16_1-MLC

Base Architecture:
Meta Llama 3.2 (1 Billion Parameters) - Instruct Tuned

Quantization:
q4f16_1 (4-bit quantization for weights, 16-bit floats for activations)
This quantization significantly reduces the model's footprint, allowing it to run smoothly in standard web browsers with WebGPU without crashing the user's device.

Estimated Download Size:
~800 MB - 1 GB (Cached in the browser after the first download)

Hardware Requirements:
- A browser with WebGPU support (Chrome 113+, Edge 113+, or Safari with feature flags enabled).
- VRAM / System Memory: Runs comfortably on devices with at least 4GB of RAM (the model itself uses around 1GB - 1.5GB of RAM/VRAM during inference).

Why this model?
1. Small Footprint: The 1B parameter size is perfect for offline browser execution.
2. Instruct Tuned: It is great at following formatting and behavior rules via system prompts, which is perfect for creating our customized KidsZone tutor personas.
3. Offline Capability: Once downloaded to the browser's IndexedDB, no internet connection is required to chat.
